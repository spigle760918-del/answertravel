# GEOFlow upstream record

AnswerTravel's frontend shell follows the design system and admin-shell interaction patterns from [GEOFlow](https://github.com/yaojingang/GEOFlow), fixed at commit `abcc638887dd35f508a59815f01ed42bc4415a09` and licensed AGPL-3.0-only.

This build changes the business routes and data contracts for AnswerTravel and excludes unrelated GEOFlow business features. The isolated deployment candidate provides its frontend corresponding source at [answertravel-v2-frontend-source.tar.gz](/answertravel-v2-frontend-source.tar.gz).

The complete license text is available at [AGPL-3.0.txt](/AGPL-3.0.txt). The corresponding-source archive is generated from the exact candidate working-tree snapshot and its SHA-256 is recorded in `CANDIDATE-MANIFEST.json` distributed with that candidate.
